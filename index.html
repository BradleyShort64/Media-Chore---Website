Technology & Approach

New tools. Old-fashioned care.

The software is not the story. How we think about your brand, how we plan the work and how we solve the problem in front of us — that is what you are actually buying.

  • We learn the business first

    Before anything is designed or shot, we want to understand how you make money, who you are talking to and what is genuinely holding the marketing back.

  • Brand management, not once-off jobs

    Look, tone and message are kept consistent across everything we touch, so each new piece of work builds on the last instead of starting again.

  • Production planned around the story

    Stills, video, aerial coverage and edits are chosen to suit the message and the setting — not to fill a shot list.

  • Problem solving over process

    Briefs change, deadlines move and campaigns need rethinking. We would rather adapt with you than hold you to a plan that has stopped working.

  • Technology where it earns its place

    AI and modern tools help with research, structure and iteration. Direction, judgement and the final call stay with the people doing the work.

  • Straight answers

    We will tell you what we can do, what we cannot, and what we think is worth your money. Nothing is promised that sits outside our control.

Questions, answered

The things people ask first.

What does Media Chore actually do?

We are a creative marketing agency in Hillcrest, Durban. We handle brand strategy and identity, website design and development, social media and content, photography and videography, SEO, AEO and GEO, and digital strategy and campaigns. In short, we take the marketing chores off your list and run them properly.

Where are you based and who do you work with?

Our base is 58 Inanda Rd, Hillcrest, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. We work with businesses, brands and entrepreneurs across South Africa, from founders starting out to people leading established companies.

What does it cost?

There are no fixed price lists. Every service is tailored and quoted according to project scope, and we build the plan around your budget rather than selling you a package you do not need.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a tool and never as a replacement for people. AI-powered workflows, modern editing, drones and current production technology let us work faster and sharper. The strategy, the creative direction and the relationship stay human.
